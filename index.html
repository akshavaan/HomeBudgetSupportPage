Privacy & Terms Summary

Home Budget With AI respects your privacy. Here is how your data is handled:

Data Collection & Usage

We only collect essential data required to provide budgeting services, such as your email address (when signing in) and expense logs you create. We never sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

Permissions

Microphone access is requested only when using AI Voice Expense logging. Speech processing is done strictly to transcribe your expense audio into structured entry data.
